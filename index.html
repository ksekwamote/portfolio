About

Engineering across the full lifecycle.

My experience crosses software development, cloud infrastructure, automation, and operational reliability. I currently contribute to national-scale systems at the Botswana Institute for Technology Research and Innovation, where dependable environments, repeatable deployments, monitoring, and long-term maintainability matter as much as the application code itself.

I design CI/CD pipelines, maintain containerised environments, configure reverse proxies and Linux servers, implement observability with Prometheus and Grafana, and work with databases that support production applications. My background in full-stack engineering means I can follow a system from interface and API design through deployment, infrastructure, monitoring, and support.

Alongside that work, I am building applied AI products and strengthening my machine-learning practice. I am most interested in AI that has a clear product boundary: structured assessments, retrieval-grounded answers, document intelligence, transcription, workflow automation, and systems that disclose their tradeoffs.

I care less about adding “AI” to a product and more about making the product measurably more useful, reliable, and honest.

verbaitim-ai.vercel.app
01 / AI transcription

Verbaitim

Accurate, exportable transcription engineered around a hard constraint: building a low-cost product entirely on free-tier infrastructure without routing large media files through serverless API limits.

  • Direct browser-to-Supabase uploads through signed URLs; Next.js only orchestrates job state.
  • Groq-hosted Whisper Large-v3 for fast transcription, with Firebase token verification on API routes.
  • Timestamp-based TXT, SRT, VTT, and DOCX exports, plus Gemini translation on demand.
  • Speaker separation is a documented gap heuristic—a disclosed tradeoff, not a hidden guess.
Next.js Supabase Storage Groq Whisper Firebase Auth Gemini

02 / Retrieval-grounded chat

Cited

A document-grounded support widget that lets a business upload its own material and embed a branded chat experience on another website through a single script tag.

  • Answers are generated from indexed source passages and returned with inline citations.
  • The customer integration surface is one script tag rather than a custom frontend project.
  • Branding and accent colour are generated per account at embed-code creation time.
  • Built for policies, FAQs, manuals, and other documents where traceability matters.

03 / Structured LLM output

Gauge

An AI-readiness assessment that scores a business across four independent dimensions instead of hiding the result behind one decorative number.

  • A six-question intake covering the business, team, bottlenecks, and desired future state.
  • Separate scores for data, team, process, and tooling make the result actionable.
  • The model must produce consistently structured output for the breakdown, quick wins, and roadmap.
  • The result includes three immediate actions and a concrete six-month implementation plan.
